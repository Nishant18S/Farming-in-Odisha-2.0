Press meet

In the news

The announcement made at the press conference held on 17 November 2026.

17 November 2026

Two-day workshop on agriculture for agri-entrepreneurs, ‘Farming in Odisha 2.0’, from December 11

UCCIL and Maruti Media are jointly organising the workshop at the UCCIL premises. It will bring together stakeholders from agriculture and allied sectors to discuss the development of agriculture and agribusiness in Odisha, said Shri Pradipta Mohanty, Chairman of the UCCIL Agriculture Committee, and Shri Manoj Prusty, Director of Maruti Media and Convener.

The conclave is a platform for experts, farmers, agri-entrepreneurs and industry representatives to discuss new opportunities, address regional challenges and explore collaboration across the agricultural value chain. Students of agriculture colleges and farmers from across the State will also take part.

Present at the press meet: Shri Brahma Mishra (immediate past President, UCCIL), Shri Murali Jamula (Treasurer, UCCIL), Shri Bibhudatta Panigrahi (Co-Chairman, UCCIL Agriculture Committee), Shri Sourav Agrawal, Shri Amiya Badjena, Shri Ananda Swain and Shri Ashok Sarada, among others.

Why we meet

Objectives

Six goals that shape the two-day conclave.

01

Promoting Innovation & Emerging Technologies

Explore advances in seeds, farm machinery, pest management and plant protection to empower agri-entrepreneurs and drive progress.

02

Strengthening Research & Industry Collaboration

Foster technical collaboration between research institutions, farmers and agri-entrepreneurs for practical adoption of innovative solutions.

03

Addressing Emerging Challenges

Examine the challenges facing agriculture in a rapidly changing environment and explore effective strategies to address them.

04

Strengthening Agricultural Supply Chains

Improve supply chain management and market linkages, and enable efficient movement and distribution of agricultural commodities.

05

Advocating Policy Support

Identify policy interventions and institutional support that strengthen agri-entrepreneurship and foster a conducive environment for growth.

06

Convention Mantras

Overcoming challenges, harvesting opportunities. Learning from the past, cultivating the future. Building a resilient, sustainable ecosystem.
