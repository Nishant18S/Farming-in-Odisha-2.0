Press meet news

The announcement made at the press conference held on 17 November 2026.

17 November 2026

Two-day workshop on agriculture for agri-entrepreneurs, ‘Farming in Odisha 2.0’, from December 11

UCCIL and Maruti Media are jointly organising the workshop at the UCCIL premises. It will bring together stakeholders from agriculture and allied sectors to discuss the development of agriculture and agribusiness in Odisha, said Shri Pradipta Mohanty, Chairman of the UCCIL Agriculture Committee, and Shri Manoj Prusty, Director of Maruti Media and Convener.

The conclave will be a platform for experts, farmers, agri-entrepreneurs and industry representatives to discuss new opportunities, address regional challenges and explore collaboration across the agricultural value chain. Students of agriculture colleges and farmers from across the State will also take part.

Present at the press meet: Shri Brahma Mishra (immediate past President, UCCIL), Shri Murali Jamula (Treasurer, UCCIL), Shri Bibhudatta Panigrahi (Co-Chairman, UCCIL Agriculture Committee), Shri Sourav Agrawal, Shri Amiya Badjena, Shri Ananda Swain and Shri Ashok Sarada, among others.
